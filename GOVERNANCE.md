# Governance

Semver Weather currently uses a maintainer-led model. Akhilesh Gogikar is the initial maintainer and release steward.

## Decisions

- Routine fixes are decided in pull-request review using tests, public/synthetic evidence, scope, security, accessibility, and maintenance cost.
- Changes to result semantics, network boundaries, governance, or supported runtimes require a public issue and an explicit maintainer decision.
- Security fixes may be developed privately and documented after coordinated disclosure.
- The maintainer can decline technically sound work that expands the v0 boundary or lacks sustainable ownership.

## Roles

Contributors submit issues, fixtures, documentation, or code. Repeat contributors may be invited to review within demonstrated areas. Commit, release, and repository-setting access remains least privilege and may be revoked when inactive or unsafe.

## Releases and succession

The release steward verifies the checklist in [LAUNCH_KIT.md](docs/LAUNCH_KIT.md), signs off the changelog, and triggers releases through reviewed tags. If stewardship changes, the successor and effective date will be recorded here before access changes.
